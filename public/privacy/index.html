Privacy, in plain language

Effective August 27, 2026. Takeout Tidy is designed so your photo library does not need to leave your device.

Photo and sidecar data

Selected photos, videos, ZIP files, JSON sidecars, hashes, repaired files, and manifests are processed inside your browser. We do not upload or receive them. Folder access is granted by your browser only after you choose a folder.

Local storage

The app stores repair preferences, a small summary of your last export, and—if activated—a license verification result in your browser. It does not store your photos in IndexedDB. You can clear this data with your browser’s site-data controls or export/import your preferences from the footer.

Network requests

The static app and its assets are fetched from this site. There are no advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party font requests. If you enter a license key, that key and this product’s slug are sent to the Sociobot billing API solely to verify access. Buying an unlock opens Sociobot’s checkout in a new tab.

Retention and control

Because photo bytes are never received by us, we cannot retain or recover them. Downloaded archives and folders remain under your control. Browser-stored preferences remain until you clear site data.

Contact

Questions may be sent through the support contact listed by Sociobot for this product.
